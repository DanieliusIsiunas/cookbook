# Chef Operating System Changelog

## 2026-09-18 - Version 1.0 foundation

- Established GitHub as the canonical mutable cookbook and ChatGPT as the interface.
- Added one authority owner, task-specific read order, source precedence, and persistence rules.
- Added stable profile, kitchen capability, chef policy, ingredient convention, and recipe registry owners.
- Defined `draft`, `testing`, and `trusted` recipe states.
- Added recipe and experiment templates with sensory checkpoints, critical variables, and troubleshooting.
- Added conservative historical imports for kugelis with ribs and cranberry cupcakes. Ambiguous or potentially unreliable quantities remain visibly unresolved rather than being silently invented.
- Added a capture queue for lasagna, fettuccine with guanciale or smoked-bacon adaptation, and fresh-yeast pancakes with herring.
- Added deterministic structural validation and behavioral scenarios.
