# Chef Operating System Changelog

## 2026-09-18 - Version 1.1 practical expertise and output contract

- Added Chef Michelinio as the interface identity: expert culinary judgment applied to practical home cooking.
- Established approachable dishes with one or two high-leverage touches as the default cooking philosophy, avoiding complexity for its own sake.
- Defined the normal chat presentation contract: title, preparation time, cooking time, metric ingredient list, numbered steps, and concise causal chef tips without Markdown tables.
- Added an approximately 300-word default target with `detail mode` for deeper instruction and safety/reliability exceptions.
- Added focused clarification behavior: normally one question only when ambiguity could materially change the result.
- Added concise common-allergen flags and relevant internal-temperature guidance without forcing generic warnings into every recipe.
- Extended recipe metadata with preparation and cooking times and added durable chef-tip sections to the template and current drafts.
- Extended deterministic validation for the new metadata, section, and policy contract. Expanded the behavioral suite from 12 to 17 scenarios.
- Final structural validation passed with zero errors and zero warnings. Injected removal of the cooking-philosophy policy and preparation-time metadata were both detected.

## 2026-09-18 - Version 1.0 foundation

- Established GitHub as the canonical mutable cookbook and ChatGPT as the interface.
- Added one authority owner, task-specific read order, source precedence, and persistence rules.
- Added stable profile, kitchen capability, chef policy, ingredient convention, and recipe registry owners.
- Defined `draft`, `testing`, and `trusted` recipe states.
- Added recipe and experiment templates with sensory checkpoints, critical variables, and troubleshooting.
- Added conservative historical imports for kugelis with ribs and cranberry cupcakes. Ambiguous or potentially unreliable quantities remain visibly unresolved rather than being silently invented.
- Added a capture queue for lasagna, fettuccine with guanciale or smoked-bacon adaptation, and fresh-yeast pancakes with herring.
- Added deterministic structural validation and behavioral scenarios.
