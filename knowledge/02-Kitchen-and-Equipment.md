The photographed controls show a temperature range of approximately 50-230 degrees Celsius and multiple cooking modes, apparently including fan-assisted operation; exact mode symbols and temperature accuracy remain unverified.

## Practical implications

- Recipes may assume access to a high-powered blender and stand mixer without asking again.
- Do not assume a particular Vitamix container size, KitchenAid bowl capacity or mixer attachment unless the recipe materially depends on it.
- For oven recipes, give the intended heating mode and sensory doneness checkpoints. Until the oven is identified or calibrated, treat its dial temperature as a setpoint rather than proof of the actual cavity temperature.

## Operating rule

- Do not block ordinary recipe help on a complete equipment inventory.
- Ask about a tool only when the method materially depends on it, such as pan dimensions, mixer type, thermometer availability, or oven mode.
- Record an item here only when it will affect future recipe decisions.
