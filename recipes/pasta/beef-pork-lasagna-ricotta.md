# Beef and pork lasagna with ricotta

## Intended result

A substantial, meat-forward lasagna with a firm but creamy ricotta layer, generous mozzarella and three layers of pasta.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 190 degrees Celsius. The successful cook's oven mode was not recorded.
2. Soften the two chopped onions and garlic in a small amount of olive oil.
3. Add the pork and beef. Cook until browned and no raw pink meat remains.
4. Add the chopped tomatoes with basil, salt and pepper. Cook until the sauce is cohesive but still moist enough to hydrate the dry pasta; the exact stovetop time was not recorded.
5. Mix the ricotta, milk, eggs, grated hard cheese, salt and pepper until spreadable.
6. Assemble three pasta layers using two Delverde sheets per layer. Keep pasta covered by meat sauce, ricotta mixture and mozzarella so exposed edges do not dry out.
7. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes at 190 degrees Celsius.
8. Remove the foil and bake for another 15 minutes, until the cheese is browned and the sauce bubbles at the edges.
9. Rest before cutting; the successful cook's resting time was not recorded.

## Sensory checkpoints

- The meat should be browned rather than grey before the tomatoes are added.
- The finished tomato sauce should spread easily without releasing a watery pool.
- A thin knife should pass through the pasta without firm resistance.
- The mozzarella should be melted and browned, with sauce bubbling around the edges.

## Chef tips

- Two eggs give the relatively small ricotta layer enough structure, while 100 ml milk keeps it spreadable.
- Keeping the tomato sauce moist and covering the dish for the first 25 minutes helps the dry pasta cook without drying the mozzarella.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| Pasta remains firm | Sauce was reduced too far, pasta was exposed or the dish was too deep | Keep every sheet covered, retain sauce moisture and extend the covered bake |
| Lasagna collapses when cut | Cut immediately or sauce was too loose | Rest before cutting and reduce only excess free liquid next time |
| Top browns before pasta softens | Dish was uncovered too early or oven top heat was strong | Keep covered for the full first 25 minutes and use pasta tenderness as the main endpoint |

## Serving and storage

Rest before portioning. Refrigerate leftovers within two hours and reheat the centre to 74 degrees Celsius.
