## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 190 degrees Celsius. The reason for later reducing the temperature is not recorded.
2. Grease the glass baking dish.
3. Prepare the potatoes and onion using the previously used but currently unresolved processing method.
4. Combine the potato mixture with eggs, sour cream, milk, butter, and verified seasoning.
5. Incorporate or layer the previously slow-cooked ribs. The canonical arrangement remains unresolved.
6. Reduce the oven to 175 degrees Celsius and bake. The historical instruction was about 70 minutes, but user feedback required about 10 additional minutes.
7. Treat roughly 80 minutes only as a historical reference. Use the sensory checkpoints and verify the centre before serving.
8. Rest before portioning; the historical rest duration is unresolved.

## Sensory checkpoints

- The exterior should be well browned rather than pale.
- The centre should be set and hot, without visibly loose potato liquid.
- A portion should hold together after resting while remaining moist.
- Ribs should be tender and evenly distributed or intentionally layered.

## Chef tips

- Treat the historical 80-minute bake as a reference, not a doneness guarantee. Dish depth and potato moisture determine how quickly the centre sets.
- Record potato weight and dish dimensions on the next cook. Those two measurements will explain more than adding extra procedural complexity.

|---|---|---|
